# BETE Protocol: The Next-Generation Trust & Evidence Network
**Architect:** Ken  
**Status:** Living Technical Whitepaper

## 1. Executive Summary
Traditional systems verify identity once. BETE verifies reality continuously. By replacing static reputation with real-time, deterministic evidence composition across every link in a transaction chain, BETE ensures trust is proven, not assumed.

## 2. BETE-GUARD: Behavioral Assurance & Duress Detection
BETE-GUARD is a covert safety net layered beneath the transaction. It ensures evidence is not just submitted, but submitted *freely*.

**Mechanics:**
* **Covert Triggers:** Hidden user signals (e.g., safe numbers, specific birthdate inputs) and a "no-hand-gesture" rule (where even a thumbs-up flags coercion).
* **Telemetry & Environmental Capture:** Simultaneous front/back camera capture, ambient audio checks, Bluetooth/WiFi proximity scanning, and accelerometer monitoring.
* **Outcome:** Mathematically scores the likelihood of coercion before execution without alerting the attacker.

## 3. ALEF (Adaptive Learning Evidence Framework)
ALEF is the "learning computer" of the protocol. It is an evolving testing matrix that doesn't just return pass/fail, but measures *information gain*.

**The 15-15-10 Matrix:**
* **15 Blue Team Tests:** Built for elite developers and deep system integrity.
* **15 Red Team Tests:** Focused on blackhat vectors, social engineering, and quantum hacking.
* **10 Baseline Tests:** Standard compliance and foundational security checks.

**Evolution:** 
ALEF continuously retires obsolete tests and strengthens valuable ones based on historical data, building an unassailable data moat over time.
